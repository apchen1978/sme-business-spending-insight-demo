# SME Business Spending Insight

A small static demo. Describe a company spending decision and get a short
plain-language explanation: what is happening, why it may matter, what to keep,
and what to ask a CPA or other professional.

- Plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript, no build step, no external calls, no persistence
- Educational wording only; not tax, accounting or legal advice
- Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with any static server

Details: `SME_BUSINESS_SPENDING_INSIGHT_V0_1_README.md`
